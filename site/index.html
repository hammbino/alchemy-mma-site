Your first class

Everyone starts somewhere. Arrive 10 minutes early wearing comfortable workout clothes. Your coach will welcome you, introduce you to the class, and help you get started.

No experience necessary. Classes combine technique, guided partner drills, and interactive training. Participation is adapted to your experience, comfort level, and the class you're attending.
