The Four7 Combat System

The method behind every class at Alchemy. Built for real fights, taught so beginners can learn it.

The system, one card at a time

Built for MMA

A combat jujitsu system made for fights where strikes are allowed. The goal is control and finishing, not points.

No gi

No lapel chokes, no sleeve grips. Everything is trained in shorts and a rash guard, the way a real fight happens.

Many arts, one system

Four7 pulls from jujitsu, wrestling, boxing, and Muay Thai, so the ground game works with the rest of your fighting.

Proven in the cage

UFC fighters from Utah, including Court McGee and Ramsey Nijem, built their ground game on Four7.

Ready for real life

Because it assumes the other person can hit you, it carries straight into self-defense.

Earned in belts

Four7 has its own belt ranks. Brian holds a Four7 brown belt.

The story behind the name, in Brian's words.

Where it comes from

Four7 was created in Utah by Jason Mertlich, a black belt who built his own combat jujitsu for mixed martial arts. It became the core of fight teams in Utah County and produced UFC fighters along the way.

Brian earned his Four7 brown belt over two decades of training and competing, and brings the system to American Fork.

Four7 Training System. Mixed Martial Movement.

How it shows up in class

  • JujitsuThe full Four7 ground game: positions, escapes, and submissions.
  • MMAFour7 with strikes added, so the ground game holds up in a real exchange.
  • Kids JujitsuThe same foundations, taught through games and drills built for kids.
