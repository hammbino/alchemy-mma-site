About Alchemy

A martial arts academy built on disciplined training, personal growth, and real community. Martial arts is the vehicle. Transformation is the destination.

What our symbol means

Every shape in the logo stands for something we train. Look closely and you will find all four.

  • The circle

    What happens within shapes what happens beyond. Step in. Stay present. Repeat.

  • The triangle

    Direction. Discipline. Will. What you build here is built on purpose.

  • Earth

    Grounded. Structured. Built. Consistency over intensity. Repetition over randomness. Stability creates strength.

  • Air

    Attention is everything. The unseen mover. What you breathe into comes alive. Guard it.

Who we are

We are

  • A place for beginners, hobbyists, and competitors
  • Built on discipline, respect, and accountability
  • Structured coaching with clear progression
  • A community where people push and support each other

We are not

  • A fight gym built on ego or intimidation
  • Hype, performative toughness, or "alpha" culture
  • Chaotic training with no structure or standards
  • Only for fighters

How it started

  1. An empty room and an ideaThe lease was signed in American Fork. The temple had a home.
  2. Training in LehiWhile the space came together, we were already rolling: jujitsu, Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays.
  3. The forge openedGrappling, striking, and open mat started in American Fork, with a free first week for everyone.
  4. GrowingKids classes, morning MMA, Muay Thai, and a fight team. Nobody arrives finished.

Strength without composure is incomplete. Skill without character is limited. Growth without discipline does not last.
