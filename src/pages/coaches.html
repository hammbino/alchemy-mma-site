Brian Thomas

Owner & Head Coach. Students know him as Coach Pirate.

Brian Thomas is a Four7 Jujitsu brown belt, former professional MMA fighter with a 9–3 record, and founder of Alchemy MMA.

With over two decades of martial arts experience, Brian has trained extensively under Coach Jason Mertlich and the principles of the Four7 System, developing a deep understanding of movement, leverage, timing, and control.

His coaching philosophy emphasizes understanding principles rather than simply memorizing techniques, helping students develop skills that adapt to real situations.

At Alchemy MMA, Brian believes martial arts is more than physical training. It's a vehicle for developing confidence, resilience, discipline, and personal transformation.

Martial arts is the vehicle. Transformation is the destination.
